# Analytical Findings

## Business Question
**How affordable is the U.S. housing market when considering home prices, mortgage rates, housing supply, and household purchasing power?**

## Status
EDA has not started. Findings will be added only after the SQL pipeline produces a validated analytical mart.

## Planned Analytical Themes
- Long-term movement in home prices.
- Changes in mortgage borrowing costs.
- Changes in housing supply.
- Household purchasing power over time.
- Periods where affordability materially improved or deteriorated.
- Whether multiple affordability pressures moved together or in opposing directions.

## Interpretation Principles
The analysis will distinguish correlation from causation, MoM from YoY change, short-term movement from long-term trend, and individual indicators from overall affordability conditions.

## Finding Format
Each major finding should document the question, evidence, observed pattern, interpretation, and limitations.
